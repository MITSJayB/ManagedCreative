About Managed Creative

Creative on the surface. Considered underneath.

We’re a creative studio in Bolton, helping businesses with websites, applications, branding and marketing.

What we care about

Great design is only the beginning. From a visitor’s first impression to the moment they make an enquiry or complete a task, every detail should have a job to do.

That’s why we start with what you’re trying to achieve, recommend what suits the job, and explain the options in plain English.

Where it started

Managed Creative is built on the foundation of Cre8ive Storm, the original web design agency where everything began.

That work grew into a broader technology focus and, in time, Managed Technology Group. Managed Creative brings it back to where it started: good design, built around what a business needs.

How we think

  • Clear by design. Thoughtful structure and messaging help people understand what you do and what to do next.
  • Made for real life. Responsive layouts, accessible thinking and straightforward editing are considered from the start.
  • Connected to your business. We consider the connections around your project, from domains and hosting to forms, integrations and ongoing care.
