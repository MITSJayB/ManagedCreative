Frequently asked questions

Which platform is right for me?

It depends on your content, functionality, budget and how you want to manage the finished project. We’ll discuss the options and recommend an approach based on what you need.

How is a custom application different from a website?

A website primarily presents information; a custom application is designed to help people complete tasks or use a service. We build applications on Cloudflare Workers, with the functionality, integrations and ongoing costs agreed for each project.

Can I update my website myself?

WordPress and Webflow both offer ways to manage website content. We’ll discuss which parts you need to update and how you’ll use them as part of your project.

Can you help with hosting and ongoing care?

Yes. We can discuss hosting through Managed Web and agree the right level of ongoing care. Hosting, platform subscriptions and maintenance are scoped separately from the build.

How much will my project cost?

Every project is different. We’ll understand what you need first, then provide a tailored proposal covering the scope, costs and any ongoing fees.

Do you work with businesses outside Bolton?

Yes. We work with businesses across the North West and can discuss projects further afield too.
